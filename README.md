## The Problem
 
In many healthcare settings, patients and clinicians experience emotional stress, burnout, anxiety, and a sense of human disconnection. Modern clinical systems prioritize efficiency and speed, often leaving limited space for emotional processing. Research shows that stress and anxiety can amplify pain perception and negatively affect recovery outcomes.
 
Current challenges include:
- Emotional burnout with few non-clinical outlets for processing
- Over-reliance on medication for pain management
- Difficulty expressing emotions verbally during illness or treatment
- Limited access to music therapy (requires certified therapists)
 
---
 
## Our Solution
 
Health Harmonies addresses these challenges through four key features:
 
### 1. Mood and Stress Check-In
Users input their emotional state at the beginning of each session to tailor their music experience based on their current mood.
 
### 2. Personalized Music Playlists
The platform generates music recommendations based on mood, preferences, language, culture, and previous listening history. Playlists vary in tempo and energy level to best fit emotional needs.
 
### 3. Guided Journal Prompts
After each music session, users reflect on how the music made them feel, helping them gain emotional awareness and better manage their emotions over time.
 
### 4. Anonymous Data Collection
Session data is collected anonymously to improve recommendations and support research while keeping personal information private.
 
---
 
## Research Methodology
 
Our research consisted of three phases:
 
1. **Background Pre-Survey** - Assessed stress levels, anxiety, and user needs
2. **Music-Based Intervention** - Users engaged with the Health Harmonies platform
3. **Post-Survey** - Evaluated the impact of the music experience and reflective activities
 
### Pre-Survey Findings (n = 17)
 
- Average stress level: 7.1/10
- Average anxiety level: 6.4/10
- 88% agree that music can reduce stress and anxiety
- 100% open to trying music to support health and emotional well-being
 
### Key Insights
 
- **Emotional Awareness Gap**: 70.6% can identify how they feel emotionally, but only 35.3% have ways to process emotions without feeling judged
- **Critical Design Insight**: 47.1% of participants could identify their emotions but did not have a safe way to process them
- **Most Desired Features**: Personalized playlist recommendations (76%), quick mood/stress check-ins (59%), short journaling prompts (53%)
- **Main Concerns**: Privacy and ease of use
 
### Platform Improvements Based on Feedback
 
Based on pre-survey results, we redesigned the platform to include:
- Fast personalized loop: check-in, personalized music, short reflection, save progress
- Simple and effortless sessions to prevent users from abandoning the platform
- Clear privacy statement explaining what is and is not tracked
 
### Outcome Measures
 
**Primary Outcomes:**
- Stress and anxiety levels rated on a 0-10 scale before and after each session
 
**Secondary Outcomes:**
- Emotional awareness rated using a Likert scale
 
**Engagement Tracking:**
- Session frequency, session length, and features used by participants
 
---
 
## Study Limitations
 
- Small sample size (n=17) limits generalizability of findings
- Most respondents were young adults (18-24 years old), so results may not reflect older adults or clinical populations
- Survey relied on self-reported responses, which may include bias
- Limited intervention testing - measured interest and needs but not long-term stress reduction
 
---
 
## Future Research
 
In the future, we plan to:
- Test the platform through a full pre-post intervention design with a larger, more diverse participant pool
- Investigate how frequency of platform usage influences stress reduction over time
- Compare the effectiveness of music with journaling versus music alone
- Expand personalization features and provide more diverse music choices
- Test effectiveness across different age groups and clinical settings
